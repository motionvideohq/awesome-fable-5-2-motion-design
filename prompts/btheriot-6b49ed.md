## Prompt

```text
Make a YouTube documentary about AI 2027 (http://ai-2027.com) that looks better, moves faster and entertains more than these three:

https://youtu.be/k_onqn68GHY (~4.9M views, small solo channel)
https://youtu.be/5KVDDfAkRgc (~11.4M views, funded team)
https://youtu.be/_g4l7YkDQwA (~22.6M views, Diary of a CEO interview)
Goal: a film you genuinely believe could match or beat their views within a year.

Before you start, write your own instructions and context files (CLAUDE.md plus whatever context you need). Work from them and keep them updated.

It's November 2026, so research what's actually happened in AI since the scenario was published. The rest is yours: angle, style, structure, length, voice.
```
